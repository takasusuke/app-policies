# Privacy Policy — Simple Todo

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This policy may be provided in multiple languages. **The Japanese version is the
authoritative version.** If there is any discrepancy between a translation and the
Japanese version, the Japanese version controls.

## 1. Introduction

Simple Todo (the "App") is a simple to-do list app: add a task, check it off. It also
offers subtasks and colored labels. This policy explains what information is collected
when you use the App and how it is handled.

## 2. Information the App Collects

### Information stored only on your device

Your tasks, subtasks, labels (name and color), completion state, and theme
(light/dark) and display language settings are stored only on your device. This
information is never sent to any external server, there is no cloud sync, and it is
deleted when you uninstall the App.

The App does not collect any personally identifiable information such as your name,
email address, phone number, or address. No account registration is required.

### Information collected by a third party for ad delivery (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner ads. Google may collect and process the
following for ad delivery, personalization, and fraud prevention:

- Advertising identifiers (iOS IDFA, Android advertising ID)
- Information about ad views and taps
- Approximate location inferred from IP address
- Technical information such as device type and OS version

This information is processed under Google's privacy policy. The App's developer does
not separately collect or retain this information.

**Tracking on iOS**: The App may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, for
example on first launch. If granted, IDFA is used to deliver personalized ads. The
App's to-do list functionality is unaffected if you decline.

## 3. What the App Does Not Use

The following are included in the App's build but are **disabled in the current
version and do not collect any information**:

- Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics — not initialized because no Firebase project is
  configured
- In-app purchases (RevenueCat) — not enabled; the App has no paid features or
  subscriptions

If either of these is enabled in the future, this policy will be updated first.

## 4. Purpose of Use

- Information stored on your device is used only for the App's operation (saving and
  restoring your tasks, labels, and settings).
- Information sent to third parties (advertising identifiers, etc.) is used only for
  ad delivery, personalization, and fraud prevention.

## 5. Disclosure to Third Parties

The information described above is processed by Google in accordance with its own
privacy policies.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google Ads Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

Information is not disclosed to any third party other than as described above.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information
from children under 13.

## 7. How to Limit Ad Personalization

- **iOS**: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → turn off "Personalized
  Ads", or set the App's tracking permission to "Ask App Not to Track"
- **Android**: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID"

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is changed, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date
revised. Significant changes will be announced within the App.
