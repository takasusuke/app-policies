## 1. Introduction

Simple Memo ("the App") is a mobile app for creating and organizing notes.
This policy explains what information is collected when you use the App
and how it is handled.

## 2. Information the App Collects

### Information stored only on your device

The content and titles of notes you create, along with settings such as
your theme (light/dark) choice, are stored only on your device. This
information is never transmitted externally and is deleted when you
uninstall the App.

The App does not collect any personally identifying information such as
your name, email address, phone number, or address. No account
registration is required.

### Information collected by a third party for advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process the
following on Google's behalf, for ad delivery and fraud prevention:

- Advertising identifiers (iOS IDFA, Android Advertising ID)
- Information about ad impressions and taps
- Approximate location inferred from IP address
- Technical information such as device type and OS version

This information is processed under Google's own privacy policy. The
App's developer does not separately obtain or store this information.

**Regarding tracking on iOS**: The App may ask for App Tracking
Transparency permission, for example on first launch, in order to deliver
personalized ads. If you grant permission, IDFA may be used to track you
across other companies' apps or websites. Granting permission is optional,
and the App works normally even if you decline — the ads you see simply
won't be personalized.

## 3. What the App Does Not Use

The following are included in the App's build but are **disabled in the
current version and do not collect any information**:

- Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics — not initialized because no
  configuration has been provided
- In-app purchases (RevenueCat) — not enabled

This policy will be updated before either of these is enabled.

## 4. Purpose of Use

Collected information is used by Google for ad delivery (including
personalization where permitted) and fraud prevention. The developer does
not use this information for usage analytics or for any purpose other
than advertising.

## 5. Disclosure to Third Parties

The information described above is processed by Google under its own
privacy policies.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google Ads Policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

No information is disclosed to any third party other than the above.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
information from children under 13.

## 7. How to Limit Ad Personalization

- **iOS**: Choose "Ask App Not to Track" on the tracking permission
  prompt when it first appears, or later go to Settings → Privacy &
  Security → Apple Advertising → turn off "Personalized Ads"
- **Android**: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID"
