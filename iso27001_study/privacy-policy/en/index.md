## 1. Introduction

27001 Study (the "App") is a flashcard/quiz app for studying information security
management concepts. This Policy explains what information is collected when you use
the App and how it is handled.

**The App is not officially produced or endorsed by any certification body or exam
organization.** It is an unofficial study aid made by an individual for learning
purposes.

## 2. Information the App collects

Study progress, accuracy records, and your settings are stored only on your device.
Nothing is sent externally, and this data is erased when you delete the App.

The App does not collect any personally identifying information such as your name, email
address, phone number, or home address. No account registration is required.

**The App currently does not display ads or offer in-app purchases, and it does not
communicate with any external server.**

## 3. What the App does not use

The following is included in the App's build but is **disabled in the current version
and collects no information**:

- Ad display (Google AdMob) — not enabled
- In-app purchases (RevenueCat) — not enabled

If we enable either of these in the future, we will update this Policy first.

## 4. Disclosure to third parties

The App does not disclose information to any third party (it currently has no feature
that communicates externally at all).

## 5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13.

## 6. Changes to this Policy

We may revise this Policy in response to new features or changes in law. If there is a
material change, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.
