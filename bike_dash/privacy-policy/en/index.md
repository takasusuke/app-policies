# Privacy Policy — Mofumofu Dash

Last updated: November 7, 2026

The Japanese version is the authoritative text.

## 1. About the app

Mofumofu Dash ("the App") is a game where animals run and jump over obstacles. Because young children may play it, the App shows ads
with **child-directed settings** (no personalized ads, only age-appropriate ads).

## 2. Information the App handles

### Stored only on your device
Records (best distance), collected coins, the selected character and settings are stored on the device and are removed when the App is deleted.
The App does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or addresses, and requires no account.

### Online rankings
To show per-character rankings, the App sends the following to Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore):
an anonymous ID issued automatically by Firebase Anonymous Authentication, a display name randomly generated by the App from fixed words
(**free text entry is not possible**), the distance run, the character used, and a timestamp.
Distance and display name are visible to other players viewing the same ranking (the anonymous ID is not). There is no chat, free text or image upload.

### Ads (Google AdMob)
The App uses Google AdMob with child-directed treatment: **no personalized ads, and ad content limited to all ages.**
The App does not track users across other companies' apps or websites and does not request tracking permission (ATT).
AdMob may process ad impressions/taps, approximate region and technical device information under Google's privacy policy.
Rewarded ads are optional.

### Stability (Firebase)
Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics collect crash reports and basic usage information. They are not linked to identifying information
and are used only to fix bugs and improve the App, not for advertising.

## 3. Not used
No in-app purchases. The App does not access location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

## 4. Sharing
Information is not provided to third parties other than the Google services above.

## 5. Children
The App is designed not to collect personal information from children (no identifying information is obtained and ranking names are not free text).

## 6. Changes
We may revise this policy; significant changes are announced by updating the "Last updated" date.
